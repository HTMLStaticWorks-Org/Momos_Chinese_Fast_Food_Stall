Golden edges.
Happy centres.

Choose your momo mood — delicate, thin-skinned steamed folds bursting with seasoned garden fillings, golden deep-fried dumplings with an irresistible crackle, or char-grilled tandoori momos brushed with spiced herb butter and fresh lime. Every plate is served with our fiery signature chilli garlic dip and roasted tomato chutney.

NOT JUST MOMOS

Wok favourites for every appetite.

High-heat wok specials tossed with fresh seasonal vegetables, aromatic spices, and signature sauces.

SPICY & SAUCY

Chilli noodles

Springy Hakka noodles flash-tossed over blazing wok heat with shredded cabbage, crunchy capsicum, burnt garlic, and our fiery house chilli sauce for a smoky street-side kick.

WOK CLASSIC

Schezwan rice

Long-grain fragrant rice tossed with crisp garden veggies, spring onion greens, and slow-simmered Schezwan pepper relish, delivering an irresistible balance of heat and savoury depth.

CROWD FAVOURITE

Chilli paneer

Golden-seared fresh paneer cubes tossed in a glossy soy-chilli glaze with crunchy bell peppers, sliced onions, and fresh coriander — the ultimate Indo-Chinese comfort pairing.

THE SECRET IS IN THE SAUCE

The dipping bar that steals the show.

Great momos are only as legendary as the chutneys they're paired with. Every batch is freshly stone-ground and simmered daily using whole spices and fresh herbs.

FIERY & BOLD
🌶️

Red Chilli Garlic Chutney

Our stall's claim to fame. Crushed red Guntur chillies, slow-roasted garlic cloves, Himalayan pink salt, and a splash of rice vinegar for a punchy, lingering heat.

Best with: Steamed Momos & Crispy Wontons
ROASTED & SAVOURY
🍅

Charred Tomato & Sesame Dip

Ripe country tomatoes charred over open flame, ground with toasted white sesame seeds, coriander roots, and mountain timur pepper for a smoky, velvety dip.

Best with: Fried Momos & Spring Rolls
COOL & CREAMY
🧄

Creamy Herb & Garlic Mayo

Silky, cooling emulsion whipped with minced garlic, cracked black peppercorns, and fresh dill. The perfect cooling counterpoint when you crave a milder indulgence.

Best with: Tandoori Momos & Spicy Bites
TANGY & SWEET
🍯

Sweet Plum & Tamarind Glaze

Simmered tamarind pulp with raw cane jaggery, crushed star anise, and toasted cumin. Sweet, tangy, and deeply aromatic for those who love contrasting flavours.

Best with: Crispy Snacks & Noodle Platters
