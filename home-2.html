CRUNCHY

Golden fried momos

Deep-fried to a crackling golden amber while keeping the savoury vegetable centre tender and juicy. Served with our signature fiery garlic chutney.

SMOKY

Tandoori momos

Marinated in a spiced yoghurt and tandoori masala blend, char-grilled for intense smokiness, and finished with melted herb butter, chaat masala and fresh lemon.

WOK FRESH

Hakka noodles

Tossed over a roaring wok flame with shredded cabbage, bell peppers, spring onions, and a splash of dark soy and aromatic sesame oil.

COMFORT

Veg fried rice

Fragrant long-grain rice wok-seared with fine-diced garden vegetables, cracked black pepper, and toasted garlic for that signature street-food aroma.
