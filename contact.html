FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about placing orders, customized party platters, dietary options, and stall timings.

How far in advance should I place a group or party order? +

For small groups (10–25 plates), 2 to 3 hours advance notice is perfect so we can steam and pack everything piping hot for your pickup. For larger catering platters or party boxes (30+ plates), please share your enquiry at least 24 hours ahead so we can reserve fresh ingredients and prep your preferred dip varieties.

Are there vegetarian and vegan options available? +

Yes, absolutely! We offer pure vegetable steamed and fried momos filled with shredded cabbage, carrots, bell peppers, spring onions, and ginger. We also offer chilli paneer, veg Hakka noodles, and vegetable Schezwan fried rice. All our main dipping chutneys (chilli garlic & tomato) are 100% plant-based.

Can we customize the spice and sauce levels? +

Yes! Just mention your preference when ordering. We can adjust the heat from mild (kid-friendly with extra sweet plum sauce or creamy dip) to medium street-style, or extra fiery Schezwan style with extra garlic and crushed red peppers.

How long does regular takeaway preparation take? +

Our steamers are kept running continuously throughout our operating hours. Walk-in steamed momos are usually served within 3 to 5 minutes, while crispy deep-fried or wok-tossed items take approximately 7 to 10 minutes from wok to box.

Do you provide disposable packaging and dipping cups? +

Yes. All takeaway orders are packed in sturdy, food-grade thermal containers with steam vents so your fried momos stay crispy and steamed momos stay tender. Dipping sauces are provided in separate sealed dip cups along with wooden forks and paper napkins.

What are your stall operating hours and peak times? +

We are open every day from 1:00 PM to 11:30 PM. Peak crowd hours are typically in the evening from 6:30 PM to 9:30 PM. If you are picking up during peak hours, we recommend calling or sending an enquiry ahead to avoid waiting in line.
